About Me

Frontend developer based in Bengaluru, originally from Hosur, Tamil Nadu. My path here is a bit unusual — I started in the automotive field as a quality inspector, which trained me to care about precision, process, and finding the root of every problem.

Those instincts now go into every component I build. I spent four years at Infosys working across platform features, version migrations, and UI decisions that genuinely moved the needle for users. Since July 2026, I've brought that same approach to TCS, in the Strategic Growth Business group, continuing to build in the Angular ecosystem.

I'm constantly learning — Angular's latest releases, how AI tooling genuinely improves developer workflows, and how good UI decisions actually reduce tickets.
